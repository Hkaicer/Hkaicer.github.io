Intrusion Detection with Suricata and an ELK SIEM

Lab project · SOC level 1 workflow

A full detection chain on a simulated LAN and WAN: Suricata inspects traffic, Logstash normalizes the events, Elasticsearch indexes them and Kibana surfaces what matters. Attacks replayed from Kali Linux and Metasploit (brute force, denial of service, data exfiltration) to verify the chain end to end, against custom Suricata signatures and Sigma rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. Alerts triaged the way a level 1 analyst would, from qualification and false positives through to a remediation plan.

lab-validated

Dynamic VPN Architecture with FortiGate ADVPN and SD-WAN

Lab project · September 2025

A secure Hub-and-Spoke design where on-demand IPsec tunnels let two remote sites talk directly instead of hairpinning through the hub, which cuts latency and hub load. SD-WAN rules steer traffic across links by availability and quality, while firewall policies allow only the flows that are needed and Virtual IPs publish selected internal services under strict source, port and protocol rules. Resilience checked by cutting links and tunnels, then watching tunnels rebuild and service hold.
